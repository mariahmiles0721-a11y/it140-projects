## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

Haunted Mansion Adventure: a spooky exploration game where the player searches an old haunted mansion for magical items.

**Storyline:**

The player is trapped inside an old haunted mansion and must explore the different rooms to collect six magical items: an Ancient Book, Silver Knife, Golden Goblet, Magic Mirror, Spell Scroll, and Crystal Ball. The player must collect all six items before entering the Attic, where a dangerous ghost is hiding. If the player enters the Attic before collecting all of the items, the ghost catches the player and the game is lost. If the player collects all six items first, the player escapes the mansion and wins the game.

## Rooms

Project One requires a minimum of eight rooms.

1. Foyer
2. Library
3. Kitchen
4. Dining Room
5. Bedroom
6. Study
7. Basement
8. Attic

## Items

With the minimum eight-room design, Project One requires at least six items. Every room except the start room and villain room must contain one item.

1. Ancient Book
2. Silver Knife
3. Golden Goblet
4. Magic Mirror
5. Spell Scroll
6. Crystal Ball

## Villain

The villain is a dangerous ghost that haunts the Attic of the mansion. The player must avoid the Attic until all six magical items have been collected.
